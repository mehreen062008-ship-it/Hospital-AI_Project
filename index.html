🏥 1. Lambi Line / Token Ka Problem

🤖 AI Solution: Smart Token System - AI mareez ko token number dega aur mobile pe batayega ke aapki bari 2:30 pe hai. Line me khare hone ki zaroorat nahi.

🤖 AI Solution: Doctor Availability App - App me likha hoga aaj kaunsa doctor available hai. Ghar beth kar appointment book kar lo.
